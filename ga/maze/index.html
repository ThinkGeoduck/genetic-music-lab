About the method. A genetic algorithm is a computational optimisation technique inspired by biological evolution — it is not a literal simulation of it, and the “genome” here is simply a list of movement instructions. Agents have no map, no sense of direction and no pathfinding: each one blindly executes its own instruction list, and fitness is measured only from where it ends up. The algorithm searches for increasingly good solutions; it is not guaranteed to find an optimal one. The shortest possible route through this maze is 62 steps.
